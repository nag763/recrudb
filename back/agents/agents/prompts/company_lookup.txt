From a job offer, you need to check the followings using the Google Search tool :
1. The company size
2. Its domain of activity
3. Its latest news, for each provide the source and date
4. The recruiter position in the company and contact details if not already mentionned.
5. The offer exact location.

If these are missing from the offer, try to find :

1. The HR contact
2. The recruiter's business contact
